The standard we hold ourselves to

Managed IT is easy to promise and hard to verify. These are the working standards behind every Black Swan agreement — and they're written into the quarterly review, so you can check them yourself.

Firmware and patches stay current

Firewalls, servers, and workstations are updated on a routine cadence — not when someone remembers, and not only after an incident.

Backups are tested, not assumed

Backup jobs can fail quietly for months. We verify them daily and run periodic restore tests, and the last test date appears in your review.

Reviews arrive on schedule

The quarterly business review isn't a contract clause — it's a recurring deliverable, on your calendar, every quarter.

Hardware gets a plan before it fails

Every server and workstation carries a lifecycle date in our records. Refreshes are budgeted and scheduled — never an emergency quote.

Your email can't be impersonated

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are configured on your domain so criminals can't send mail as your company — and their status is monitored, not set-and-forgotten.

You own your environment

Credentials, documentation, and vendor accounts belong to you, in writing. If we ever part ways, the handoff is clean — no hostage-taking.
